Option C: Status board

Status leads. Three columns, To do, In progress and Done, so what is moving and what is finished is the shape of the page. Inside To do, cards group as Overdue, Today, Tomorrow and Later. A band of goal meters runs above the board. A card moves to another status by a drag across columns, and to another day by its chip. A phone shows one column at a time under three tabs.

Sample date: Thursday 26 November 2026. Same shapes as option A: empty, half and full box; a black "!" chip is overdue.
