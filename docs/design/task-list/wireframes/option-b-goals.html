Option B: By goal

Goals lead. Each goal is a section with its progress bar and due line at the top, then its open tasks sorted by when they are due: overdue first, then today, tomorrow and later. Tasks with no goal come last in their own section. One line of counts at the top says what is overdue, due, in progress and done today. A task moves to another day with its chip and to another goal by a drag.

Sample date: Thursday 26 November 2026. Same shapes as option A: empty, half and full box; a black "!" chip is overdue.
