How the controls reach the rig

Shape scales every pose toward the resting circle, so at 0 nothing ever leaves round. Eyes sets face size and whether a squint is allowed. Colour sets how far the body warms at the top of the wave, or cools while it zooms. Motion switches the extras on and off: the anticipation dip, the hand lagging on the way down, the tuck past rest, the landing pop, the bob and the spin grooves. On the wheel, playful adds wave cycles, squash, bob and tempo; serious removes them and keeps the eyes round. Candid raises the arm higher, holds it a beat and zooms faster; deferential shortens the reach and adds a small bow at the end. Distance from the centre is how much of all that applies.

The scenery during a zoom is not part of Jet. It reads the clip's speed track, draws in the ink colour, and fades away at rest.
