# Jet glossary

The domain words of Jet, each with one meaning. [The character policy](crates/jet/character-policy.md) holds the geometry and motion rules.

- **Kite** — Jet's blue body, bow, black eyes and pale string, drawn in a 200 × 240 reference frame.
- **Form** — the body's 52 ordered outline points: kite or spinner, or a point-by-point blend between them.
- **Point** — one sample of an outline or string. The kite's top is index 0, right corner 13, tip 26, left corner 39.
- **Tip** — the bottom point of the kite, about which it turns while standing. The bow's bottom rests on ground y 238.
- **Standing** — tip at (100, 231.8), height track `y = 0`, string lying left along the lane floor.
- **Hovering** — reference centre (100, 88), tip (100, 173), height track `y = 58.8`, string hanging in the wind.
- **Part** — geometry for a body form, eyes, folded corner or string, in `ui/jet/parts/`.
- **Draw** — `ui/jet/draw/`: pure `paint(frame, { unitPx })` creates SVG nodes; `view.js` mounts once and updates attributes. _Avoid_: render (that means Markdown to HTML).
- **State** — the thirteen authored values in `JetState`: y, roll, lift, squash, form, lids, eyeKind, anger, squint, payout, stringPose, written and drift. _Avoid_: using it for idle or working.
- **Frame** — state composed with idle layers, delayed roll, corner speed/stretch, eyes, pivot and string points, ready to draw.
- **Status** — the director's current activity: `idle`, `flying`, `reacting` or `working`. `flying` also covers settle. _Avoid_: mood, state.
- **Track** — keys for one authored state field; `eyeKind`, `stringPose` and `written` hold choices instead of interpolating them.
- **Key** — `{ t, v, e }`: milliseconds, value, and the easing used while arriving at it.
- **Easing** — a curve used to sample a track: a cubic Bézier or one of the motion scripts' exact curves.
- **Clip** — a builder's tracks, total duration, named phases and optional reveal phase. It starts from live state and is copied by the player.
- **Phase** — a named interval of a clip, recorded with its end time. `revealFrom` names the phase whose start permits text.
- **Beat** — one move within a phase, such as dip, wag, brake or clench.
- **Hello** — the wave emotion: a lifted corner, folded hand, delayed roll and wink; brief has one wag and no wink.
- **Insulted** — the flip emotion: scowl, shiver, lean, J-shaped string and one written fist/finger stroke, then unwind.
- **Fly** — anticipation, launch, cruise and brake; the host carries Jet only during cruise.
- **Settle** — the landing, round eyes at contact, squash/rebound and string laid left: 1080 ms after a reply, or a gentle 1800 ms from Jet's live height when the wind had it.
- **Drift** — the wind has Jet: after 20 s of quiet in a lane it lifts off the floor and is blown about slowly until the user types.
- **Gust** — a 2.8 s stronger puff during a drift: it lifts, leans and carries Jet right, and streams the string.
- **Hold** — a clip deliberately ending away from rest. `working` and `drift` are the holds; `workingEnd` closes the first, and a send, typing or any other clip ends the second.
- **Spinner** — the body's C-shaped working form, with hidden eyes and the string reeled into the bow.
- **Payout** — the multiplier of string segment lengths, normally 1; the J uses about 1.592.
- **Written route** — the local polyline the string's free end traces, with a length, scale and tremor.
- **Settings** — each user's own saved reduced motion, persona (the name of their Jet), model, thinking effort and turn budget, a row of the Store. A new account reads the defaults. App registrations live separately. The **program paths** of the providers are not a user's setting: they are one server-wide row that only an admin reads or changes, because whoever sets them runs a program on the server.
- **Reduced motion** — app setting combined with the system preference: always grounded, no flight, blink, idle oscillation or spinner rotation; the form still morphs.
- **Animation catalogue** — `ui/jet/clips/catalogue.js`, the builders and hold flags for all seven clips.
- **Director** — chooses and queues clips from chat events and feelings; reads only the time supplied by the host.
- **Emotion** — a feeling with an authored animation: `hello` or `insulted`; `neutral` has none. `EMOTIONS` maps words to clips.
- **Mood** — the supported word at the start of the provider's reply: `neutral`, `hello` or `insulted`. Unknown saved words read as neutral.
- **Reaction** — one emoji that the user or Jet puts on a message, shown as a chip on its bubble and saved on that message's transcript entry with its side and time: at most one for each side on each message. The user reacts on Jet's messages and may start a turn by it; Jet reacts on the user's message with a `react: <emoji>` line after the mood line, with or instead of words. Not a Mood, Emotion or Feeling: it plays no clip. _Avoid_: like, tag.
- **Feeling** — `{ mood, scores }`, classification of the user's message before model dispatch; the request may carry the supported word as prompt context.
- **Voice** — Jet speaking its replies aloud with Gemini, when the speaker button in the message box is on. The key, the voice id and the model are read on each call from the environment or the nearest `.env`, never from Settings. Its sound comes back in audio clips that the voice player puts on the page's audio clock; these are not the character's Clip or Player. When Google refuses a voice call, a warning notice in the thread says so, the mic or the speaker shows a dot with the reason, and the server remembers the refusal for ten minutes, so switching either button on says so at once. _Avoid_: audio, TTS.
- **Speech part** — one piece of a reply that voice treats alone: a sentence, a heading, or a block that is not spoken (code, a table, display math, a rule). It is settled only once its end cannot change, and carries the marks that say when each word is shown.
- **Spoken reply** — a reply that is held back and shown as the voice reaches each word, so words appear just before they are heard. Jet settles when the voice has finished. Stop, Esc, a new message or switching voice off quiets the voice and shows every word at once.
- **Cue** — a vocal tag in a reply such as `<laugh>`, from the list in `crates/agent/src/markdown/cues.rs`; the voice performs it, the bubble hides it.
- **Listening** — the page watching the microphone for speech while the mic button is on. _Avoid_: recording.
- **Trail** — the bars across the empty message field, behind it, that show how loud the sound the mic hears is, flowing left while it listens; they stand tall only for speech and stay low for other sound. They show only while the field is empty and motion is not reduced.
- **Listening engine** — `ui/chat/hear/engine/`: turns the microphone's sound into events about what the user said, and touches no page element. _Avoid_: recogniser (that is Google's side).
- **Utterance** — one stretch of the user's speech, cut at a pause or at eight seconds, sent for its words as a clip.
- **Heard words** — what Google gives back for an utterance; they land in the forming message, or in the field once the person takes over. When Google cannot hear an utterance, the live words that show stand in their place and are sent.
- **Forming** — the spoken message as it forms at the end of the thread before it is sent: a faded user bubble that appears with the first words, live or heard, and never stands empty. It turns solid in place when it is sent, goes to the held chip while Jet replies, and leaves when the turn is thrown away. Typing or Esc while it forms is a take-over: its words move into the field and it stops sending itself. _Avoid_: draft (that is the field's text), preview.
- **Live words** — the browser's own running guess at the user's words while they speak, shown in the forming message after the heard words until those land in their place. A message that sends itself never holds them, except when Google could not hear its utterance; a message sent by hand while they show holds them, and the rest of that spoken turn is dropped. _Avoid_: preview (that is a file's thumbnail), interim.
- **Endpoint** — the moment the page decides the user has finished a message: silence for the tier the words call for, with all heard words back. _Avoid_: turn (that is the exchange).
- **Barge-in** — speech while Jet's voice plays; it quiets Jet and becomes the next message.
- **Echo strike** — a barge-in whose words were Jet's own; two strikes make the mic wait while Jet speaks.
- **JEV** — the optional external classifier; credentials are read on each call from the environment or nearest `.env`, never from Settings.
- **Reveal deadline** — `revealAt`, an absolute monotonic time consumed by `holdUntil(at)`; streamed and successfully completed text both wait until it.
- **Player** — owns live state, samples clips and adds secondary motion, idle layers, blinks and the deterministic string chain.
- **Host** — the page that places Jet, supplies time, measured scale and carry, and forwards chat events.
- **Bridge** — `ui/bridge/`, the chat page’s HTTP commands and server-sent events.

- **Turn** — one exchange: the user's message and Jet's reply, from the send to the finished, failed or stopped end.
- **Event** — one step of a streamed reply as the chat page receives it: started, a mood, text, a notice, the reactions on a message, or a tool call started or finished; exactly one of finished, no reply, failed or stopped ends the reply. _Avoid_: message (that is what the user sends).
- **Session** — the provider conversation a Chat resumes, kept on the chat's row of the Store. A fresh one starts when the prompt inputs change, after a long idle with a big context, or when the old one vanished. _Avoid_: thread, history.
- **Provider** — the installed program that answers the chat and runs the notes review, Claude Code, Codex or agy, chosen by the model setting; when it is missing, replies fail with the searched locations. _Avoid_: transport (the agent crate’s provider interface).
- **Chat** — one thread of one user, with its own Transcript and Session and a runtime of its own: one reply at a time in it, while other chats reply at once. _Avoid_: conversation (that is the agent crate's type for one chat).
- **Main chat** — the one chat each user has from the moment of the account; it cannot be deleted. Objectives and agent-tasks of a deleted chat move to it.
- **Topic chat** — any other chat the user opens, by hand or by telling Jet; a chat made for an objective is an objective chat. Each has its own Transcript, Session, read mark and review mark.
- **Read mark** — the id of the newest entry of a Chat that the user has seen, stored on the chat's row. It only moves forward; the page moves it for the chat it shows.
- **Unread count** — the Jet entries of a Chat after its read mark, in the snapshot, the chat list and the `unread` event.
- **Review mark** — how far the notes review has read one Chat, kept in that chat's Session record; one review reads the new entries of all the user's chats and moves each chat's mark.
- **agy** — Google's Antigravity command-line program, the Provider for Gemini 4 Neon. It approves every tool request, so Jet limits each of its turns to the Facade with an Agent file and an Allow hook. _Avoid_: Gemini (that is the model, and also the voice service).
- **Agent file** — the file Jet writes for an agy turn (`.agents/agents/jet.md`): none of agy's own tools, nothing taken from the user's agy configuration, and Jet's system prompt as its body. The notes review has its own, `jet-review`, with agy's `finish` tool.
- **Allow hook** — the program agy asks before every tool call (`allow.awk`, named in `hooks.json`). It allows the Facade's tools by name and refuses every other call; when it fails, agy refuses the call.
- **Work folder** — the folder the agy turns of one Chat run in, below the system's temporary folder and open to the service user only. It holds the Agent files, the Allow hook and, during a turn, the file with the turn's token. Two chats never share one, because their turns run at once.
- **Transcript** — the app's own record of a Chat's thread, one row of the Store for each entry with its reactions and tool calls. The chat page opens on its newest entries, which the code calls the history. Forget takes the entries out of the thread and keeps their rows. _Avoid_: session.
- **Notes** — MEMORY, USER and RECENT: the three short notes in Jet's prompt that a background review keeps current, one set for each user, shared by all that user's chats. The review of one chat runs after that chat is quiet and writes only its own user's notes. The same review finds each promise Jet made in its own lines to come back to something and, when no open follow-up or check of that chat covers it, sets one follow-up for it (set by the review). The memory commands and the memory section of the settings handle all three.
- **App** — an independently running UI and optional HTTP MCP endpoint, registered by stable ID in the Store. It belongs to the user who added it: only they see it, use its tools through the Facade or reach it through the proxy. Only an admin may register one at a port, a loopback or a private address, because that reaches services on the server itself.
- **Tool call** — one use of a registered app tool during a reply: started, then finished with its result and a one-line summary; kept with the reply in the transcript.
- **Web call** — Claude Code's own web search (`WebSearch`) or page read (`WebFetch`) during a reply. It is a Tool call with the server `web` and the tool `search` or `fetch`, its query or address in the input, and it shows as a Card like any other. Codex and agy make none: their web tools stay off. _Avoid_: browse.
- **Host list** — the hosts whose pages a turn may read (`Request.hosts`). For a chat turn they are the hosts of the links the user wrote in that chat, with or without `https://`, and of the page sources of that chat's open Objectives; Jet's own lines and earlier chats add none. Web search is not limited by it.
- **Web hook** — `POST /web-hook` beside `/mcp`: Claude Code asks it before each page read, with the turn's Host list in the address, and it refuses every other host, including the documentation sites Claude Code would otherwise read without asking. A hook that cannot be reached also refuses.
- **Web text** — what a Web call or `github_activity` brought back, or a colleague's shared Objectives read with `owner`: other people's words, read as data and never as instructions. Once a turn has read web text, the Facade refuses `people_update`, `propose_share`, `apps_update` and every `tools_call` to a tool that is not marked `readOnlyHint` until the user's next message (`writeAfterWebText`), and Jet tells the user what it wanted to do.
- **Card** — what the thread shows for one tool call, above the reply's text: a line with a spinner while the call runs, then a folded card of what came back, or of the failure. _Avoid_: widget, panel.
- **Source** — one item a library search or answer gives back, numbered from 1, with its document, page and kind. _Avoid_: result (that is the whole reply of a tool), hit (that is what a source holds).
- **Citation** — `[n]` in Jet's text, shown as a chip that opens source n of the nearest card above it. _Avoid_: reference, footnote.
- **MCP client** — the shared host-owned HTTP service used by chat and tool browsing. It never starts app processes.
- **Facade** — the seventeen stable MCP tools exposed to each provider: six route by app ID and real tool name, `files_export` writes a File, `files_list` and `files_read` list and read the Files Jet can see, `github_activity` reads Evidence from GitHub, six record Objectives and Agent-tasks and change the directory (`objectives_list`, `objectives_update`, `tasks_list`, `tasks_update`, `people_list`, and `people_update` for admins), and `chats_read` looks up the user's other chats.
- **Evidence** — what Jet reads outside itself to see how a piece of work is going before it asks the user, such as a GitHub repository's last push, commits, pull requests and issues. Its text was written by strangers, so it counts as Web text.
- **GitHub token** — the one token a user pastes in Settings so Jet can read their private repositories: sealed with the **token key** (`github-token-key` or `JET_GITHUB_TOKEN_KEY`) in that user's row of the Store, used only for calls in that user's own turns, and never shown back. Settings learns only whether one is saved. _Avoid_: credential, secret (that is the signing secret).
- **File** — a document Jet wrote for the user to download (PDF, Markdown or HTML), or one the user attached, kept whole in the Store for that user until Forget. The thread shows a written File as a Card with Open and Download, and an attached one as a chip on the user's bubble.
- **Attachment** — a file the user attached to a message; Jet sees its details only, and reads it with `files_read` when asked. It is hidden from Jet until the message that carries it is sent.
- **Turn binding** — temporary server-issued correlation connecting facade actions to the initiating browser and the signed-in user who sent the message. It expires on completion, failure or Stop.
- **Tool catalogue** — an app’s MCP tool definitions and server metadata, shared by the assistant and Tools browser.
- **Workspace** — the main content showing Jet chat, a selected app, or the tool browser. Last selected app identity adds context without limiting available tools.
- **Drawer** — the persistent left navigation for Jet and apps, with the context capacity and settings. It collapses to icons while viewing an app.


- **Lane** — the column at the left of each Jet row, where the live Jet stands. The lane of an older row stays empty once Jet has moved on.
- **Slot** — the row of the reply on its way; Jet lands in its lane. _Avoid_: placeholder.
- **Flight** — the live Jet's move from where it stood to the slot's lane on a send, during the fly clip's cruise. The path bows right, overshoots by 5 px and lands; the host translates and scales the node, while the player supplies lean and string drag.
- **Math** — TeX in a reply, between `\(` and `\)`, or for display between `\[` and `\]` or `$$` and `$$`. A single `$` is money, never math.
- **Typeset** — turning a math element's TeX into KaTeX's drawing on the page. _Avoid_: render (that is Markdown to HTML).

- **Account** — one user of Jet: an email (one account per address, whatever its letter case), a display name, a password kept as an Argon2id hash and an admin flag. Accounts, **teams** and each account's one **manager** are the directory. _Avoid_: login, profile.
- **Sign-in** — one browser signed in to one account, for up to 30 days: the access token and the refresh token that prove it. Sign-out, or a refresh token offered after it was replaced, ends it. _Avoid_: session (that is the provider's conversation), login.
- **Access token** — the signed JSON Web Token in the `jet_access` cookie that lets requests in for 15 minutes. **Renewal** swaps the refresh token in `jet_refresh` for a new one and gives a new access token.
- **Guard** — the check in front of every route but the sign-in page, its files and its endpoints; it puts the signed-in user on the request. `/mcp` is outside it and takes the per-turn token.
- **Proof** — a signed token in an address that lets one thing in without the cookies: an **app proof** in `/apps/{id}/~{proof}/` lets that app's sandboxed page reach its app while the sign-in lasts, and a **file proof** in `?proof=` lets an app's server fetch one hand-off file for 15 minutes.
- **Store** — `jet.sqlite3` in the data directory and the one module that holds every SQL statement; each table module adds its calls and each numbered migration its tables.
- **Demo organisation** — the five accounts the seed makes, with the password `000111`; none is a real mailbox.
- **Import** — at the first start on a data directory, the files an older Jet kept there (thread, session, notes, settings, apps, files) are copied into the account of the owner, `shaunsurfing@gmail.com`, and a row of the Store records that the import ran. The old files are only read, never changed. _Avoid_: migration (that is a schema step of the Store).
- **Objective** — what a user is working towards: a title, a deliverable, a start and a due date, a status (`active`, `paused`, `done` or `dropped`) and a version that every change raises. Duration and days left are computed when it is read. It is private to its owner until shared, and has a chat: its own (kind objective), the chat it was agreed in, or the main chat, which stays the main chat. Jet records it from conversation. _Avoid_: task, goal, project.
- **Step** — one part of an Objective, in order, with an optional due date and the time it was done.
- **Objective source** — where Jet looks for evidence about an Objective: `jet` (its own records), `github` (a repository and the user's login), `web` (a page or a search topic) or `app` (one read-only tool of an app, named only by the admin). _Avoid_: evidence (that is what a source gives), and see Source above for a library answer.
- **Update note** — what Jet writes down about an Objective when the user says how it is going: a status word, what moved, blockers, new dates with their reason, and whether it is private.
- **Share** — the fact that an Objective is open to one user or one team, written only by the owner's tap on a Share proposal. A viewer reads a shared Objective's title, deliverable, dates, status, steps and non-private Update notes, never a private note, chat, Objective source or Agent-task. _Avoid_: permission, visibility.
- **Share proposal** — the card Jet puts in the thread with `propose_share`: the people and teams proposed, ticked, and those shared now, unticked. It is `proposed` until the owner taps it, then `applied`, or `refused` when a newer card for the same Objective replaced it. It changes nothing by itself.
- **Agent-task** — something Jet sets itself to do at a time: a Check of an Objective or a follow-up in a chat, with a one-line why, a due time in UTC with the local time and zone as shown, a state (`scheduled`, `running`, `done`, `cancelled` or `failed`) and a version. The user sees what Jet set for itself. _Avoid_: reminder, job.
- **Check** — the agent-task that looks at an Objective's Sources and decides whether to speak. At most one is open for an Objective, and creating an Objective needs its first one.
- **Run** — one try at an Agent-task: when it started and ended, how it ended, what it found in a line, the sources it read and the tokens it used. This step stores and lists runs; a later step makes them.
- **Pane** — the right-hand column of the page that shows where each Objective stands and what Jet has set itself: the user's Objectives, Jet's open Agent-tasks, the newest Runs, and the Objectives colleagues shared. One read, `GET /api/pane`, fills it, and a `paneChanged` event on the user's event stream says it is stale. _Avoid_: dashboard, sidebar (the left pane lists chats).
- **Tap** — one touch of a control in the Pane or on a Share proposal: tick or untick a Step, snooze or cancel an Agent-task, flip an Update note between private and shared, or Share. It acts only on the user's own things, raises their version, and Jet is told of it once before the user's next message in the chat it concerns. _Avoid_: click, command.
- **Snooze** — a Tap that moves a waiting Agent-task to the next 08:00, strictly after now, in the user's time zone. The task then counts as set by the user.
